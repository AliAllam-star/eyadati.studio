Why Did We Start?

In a world going digital, we noticed many Arab medical clinics have exceptional medical expertise but lack a digital presence worthy of their status. Ready-made websites were either too complex or didn't suit clinics.

That's where Eyadati Studio was born. We decided to design website templates specialized 100% for clinics, combining beauty, simplicity, and ease of customization.

What Do We Offer?

We don't just sell code, we design a complete experience. Every template is built carefully to suit its specialty and is fast, responsive, and easy to modify.
