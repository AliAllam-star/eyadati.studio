Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

At Eyadati Studio, we prioritize the privacy of our visitors and customers. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website or purchase our templates.

1. Data We Collect

We collect two types of data:

  • Data you provide: Such as name, email, and phone number when contacting us or making a purchase.
  • Payment data: All payments are securely processed directly through our payment gateway Gumroad. We do not store or access your credit card information on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to:

  • Complete purchases and deliver your digital files (templates).
  • Communicate with you regarding your orders or technical inquiries.
  • Send updates or promotions (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Improve the quality of our services and website.

3. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Gumroad: To process payments and deliver files.
  • Service providers: Such as email or hosting services, solely to operate the site.

4. Data Security

We take appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. However, 100% security of data transmission over the internet cannot be guaranteed.

5. Cookies

Our site and Gumroad may use cookies to enhance your experience and remember preferences (like dark mode preference).

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, modify, or delete your personal data. Contact us to do so.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, contact us at: hello@eyadati.studio
